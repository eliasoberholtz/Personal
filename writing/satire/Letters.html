Against Gingers

Reginald of Haroldshire, 1902

To my brother,

Wolrich[1], I am writing this letter to you because I have seen an uptick in the amount of red-headed devils in our beloved town. It is ridiculous, and indeed, morally insane, for our present government to allow more immigration from those nations of native ginger-folk. Unlike that of the French, these races of gingers are useless. They possess no talent and produce nothing of value. Their pitiful State, 'Filoðaz', so they call it, relies on imports from its neighboring States. Their only major general, and the greatest, died a thousand years ago in his first battle, which they lost. They are, as the general[2] said, 'devoid of thought'. Because of this lack of reasoning capability, it is evident that they are subhuman, and evidently reprobate as Our Lord permits only men and the angels (and dogs) in the gates. A distinction afforded only to man is the ability to reason, and as they cannot, they are not human at all. Their head appears to be a gelatinous blob and more deformed than a monkey.

Footnotes

  1. Wolf + rich, from Old English ƿulf and rīċe.
  2. Nobody knows who he is referring to.
