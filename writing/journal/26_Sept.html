Mea culpa mea culpa mea maxima culpa

8th September

Misery! Every day is evidence to my failure: failure in the love of friends, on one hand, and of God, on the other. Indeed, it was my own villainous vices that made a good friend leave, and, because he's friends with the others, I am left alone. It is punishment for my complete and utter idiocy.

Further, it is proof to my own undesirableness. If I cannot be friends or make friends, how can it be assumed that I can love a woman as myself? It certainly does not follow. Friendship is a lower form of society and has to work for the common good, but it's a special bond. While dying for a fellow man in society is good (wars in defense), death for one's friends is a different matter, a higher good. When a friend breaks those bonds of friendship, he almost, in a way, acts as a tyrant. The Doctor angelorum says: 'Rather, tyrants, who by greater domination incite discontent and rebellion in the people subject to them, are the rebels. For governance is tyrannical when ordained for the ruler's own good to the detriment of his people.'[1] But because a friendship is, in a sense, a polity, which is a worse form of governance than that of a monarch, how much worse is a tyranny in the polity? What will my punishment be on judgement day for my crime against the friendly bond? What I am suffering here on Earth is nothing but a grain of sand to my just punishment awaiting my soul in the depths of reprobation.

And, now, to return to the topics of an even greater bond: between a husband and a wife. I reiterate my qualities from what I've said elsewhere: I'm miserable, I'm not particularly handsome, incapable of social interaction with them, and, even then, I will not be able to support a family. When I leave college, I aim to become a teacher, which is a profession not known for good pay. Even worse, because I live in California, I will not be able to afford a house, and will hardly be in a financial standing to afford children, much less the amount I wish to have (five at least). I'm being lead into a slavery, if I'm not already a slave to horrid passions.

Lord, why do you permit me to continue life if I am already dead? You would be justified in killing me now, as you did the wicked men in the Flood! I am grime and scum upon what otherwise is a beautiful and lush Earth.

My Reflections Continued

10th September

I was intending to make some calculations concerning the cost of a family of around 7 (five children and two parents) but I am currently unable to do so. I'll append the calculations to the end.

What I intend to discuss in this are further reflections regarding marriage and future prospects on that question.

My first issue is 'Whether I am fit for religious life or to marriage'? The rule considering this issue is found in Canon Law and in the letter to Timothy. The two primary rules being celibacy in the priesthood as well as being able to hold habits and discuss at length with parishioners. As is plainly clear from my last reflection on Tuesday, I have a very low 'social battery' and I can become quite irritable after a while. Neither aspects are good for a holy priesthood. Moreover, the priest ought to have the ability to form a habit of prayer easily. I am poorly suited to the formation of good habits.

Marriage on the other hand, is a bond between a man, a woman, and God. Part of this is for the purpose of mutually working for each other's salvation. Where I fail in forming habits, a loving wife will aid me, and vice-versa. My priest discusses with maybe 1,000 members or more, and I can hardly deal with more than 2-5. Therefore, it is clear that the ideal qualities of the priesthood are ill-suited for me, nor am I worthy to do such holy work (even if some heretics defile the Holy Church with blasphemies).

Next, I ought to discuss the ideal family and its life. The ideal family ought to be large and close together. First, concerning large families. It is evidence of a special bond between a man and his wife, especially in this modern era where the replacement rate is higher than the current fertility rate. Additionally, a family that has many children and prays together are of more loving and less likely to divorce. A family that stays together is also happier, and more likely to help each other. In a later reflection, I will respond to the anti-natalists.

Interruption in the Last

11th Sept

I decided to interrupt my train of thought from yesterday's reflection and discuss a more despairing topic. I was, clearly, not alive then.

  2. Experiences Morning

I woke up this morning at about 5:55 or 5:50, although my clock said 6:00 because it is about ten minutes ahead (too lazy to change it). I turned on my computer and sat for some time when the wi-fi turns on. I knew that today was when @2001Live was posting about the event as he was seemingly building up for the events for weeks. 2001Live is an account that does a lot of research in reporting the news in the perspective of a reporter in that year. Anyway, the wi-fi was turned on and I logged into that infuriating (and demonic) wasteland that was Twitter, X, or Xitter. If I am remembering correctly, the account's post was the first on my feed. It was something about the second tower and what was happening there. The attack had started very close to when I woke up, maybe twenty or thirty minutes before. Many posts were being made in minute-two minute intervals. It was awful! I had to go read something else because I couldn't handle it at several points. I nearly cried several times, maybe around 6:30 and at 6:55.

Marginal Note: Maybe the first report I saw was Pres. Bush at the school.

This morning was the first time I was hearing about several details. Firstly, and the most awful to me, were the people jumping off the towers following the attack. Secondly, were the awful and gruesome details of the bodies following. One report read that eyes came out of the sockets during tagging and identification. Thirdly, I had never heard nor read recordings nor transcriptions of victims and their messages. Fourthly, I was not aware of the car bombings in the National Mall and in the Capital. There were other details I was not aware of but those are the primary ones.

Perhaps I never paid attention when they discussed the event in school, or maybe they never wished to discuss such details to us students (I'm 17). Whatever the case, I was unaware of the details and found the topic too horrible to investigate. Taboo or something.

Marginal Note: They did not dwell or give a moment of silence this year in school.

What first piqued my interest in the subject this year in particular (2026) was that I had watched a video discussing some of the conspiracies of the event.[2] It was an interesting video - and there are many interesting conspiracies. I might investigate further and might not, but whoever caused such went on my country and on many young lives is awful. May Christ have mercy on the souls lost. Whatever and whoever caused it is certainly evil. Those are my final thoughts for today. I hope tomorrow brings a brighter reflection.
